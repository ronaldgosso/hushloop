2. Windows 11 Audio & Distraction Silencing
HushLoop interacts with Windows Multimedia audio endpoints (winmm.dll) solely
during active user focus sessions to silence external auditory distractions.
A write-ahead crash ledger protects system audio state and automatically
restores master volume in the event of an unexpected termination or system suspend.
