# Brag Plan: HushLoop

## What is this app?
HushLoop is an active Pomodoro desktop timer for Windows 11 featuring automated distraction silencing, zero-asset procedural Web Audio soundscapes (432 Hz alpha waves & rain), and a drift-resistant epoch timer wrapped in a Fluent dark glassmorphic shell.

## The angle
"Your timer shouldn't drift when your laptop sleeps, and focus shouldn't require downloading 2GB of audio loops." HushLoop turns the desktop Pomodoro into a high-precision, crash-resilient acoustic sanctuary designed specifically for Windows 11 power users.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Deep obsidian canvas ignited by a breathing cyan aurora (`#00F2FE`). The bold statement slams in: **"Time to quiet the noise."** The circular neon gauge powers up with an orbiting comet and snappy clock tick.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Zero-Drift Engine**: Real-time timestamp delta tracking that laughs at sleep mode and OS minimization.
- **Active Distraction Silencing**: Windows Multimedia (`winmm.dll`) volume ducking backed by a Write-Ahead Crash Ledger.
- **Zero-Asset Soundscapes**: Procedural Web Audio API synthesis generating dual 432 Hz binaural alpha waves and pink-noise rain in pure math, zero MP3 downloads.
- **Dynamic Phase Shift**: Seamless glow transition from Electric Cyan Focus (`#00F2FE`) to Coral Recovery (`#FF4B72`).

## Outro / punchline
The HushLoop acoustic loop logo locks in with the floating dock:
**"Deep work, uninterrupted. Built for Windows 11."**
Ronald Gosso attribution & GitHub release badge settle cleanly.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Launch & Activate**: Hit Play on the floating glass pill dock; the electric cyan neon ring lights up and procedural 432Hz soundscape fades in.
2. **Distraction Silenced**: Active silencer engages with "Write-Ahead Crash Ledger" safeguard indicator.
3. **Session Cycle Transition**: Progress reaches 100%, bell chime triggers, and the gauge shifts into warm coral short break.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Spark & Neon Ignition — 4.0s
- **Visual**: Living Aurora background slowly ripples over obsidian space. A glowing neon badge pulses: `WINDOWS 11 NATIVE`. Centered headline slams in: **"DEEP FOCUS, SILENCED."**
- **Action**: The circular gauge renders at `25:00`. A simulated cursor clicks the central play toggle on the floating dock.
- **Animation**: Dash-offset animates, cyan neon glow bursts (`#00F2FE`), and the comet particle starts orbiting the perimeter.
- **Sequential/interaction**: Cursor click animation with ripple wave -> instant neon illumination.
- **Audio intent**: Tactile mechanical click followed by a low sub-bass bloom and subtle electric hum.
- **Audio-coupled idea**: Button click SFX precisely on cursor tap (t=1.2s), followed by neon surge.
- **Transition mood**: Smooth cinematic zoom-wipe into UI close-up -> Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Procedural Soundscapes (Zero-Asset Web Audio) — 5.0s
- **Visual**: Focus shifts to the floating glass pill dock. The audio popover opens with a frosted glass spotlight card.
- **Options displayed sequentially**:
  - `[Waves] Alpha Waves (432Hz)` (Selected with active cyan dot)
  - `[CloudRain] Rain Ambience`
  - `[Disc] White Noise`
- **Overlay Callout**: **"Zero MP3 Downloads. Pure Web Audio API Math."**
- **Sequential/interaction**: Popover slides up (t=0.4s), soundscape pill highlights with glowing checkmark (t=1.6s), frequency wave visualizer breathes.
- **Audio intent**: Soft binaural 432Hz acoustic tone enters the music mix, proving real-time synthesis.
- **Transition mood**: Fast slide wipe -> Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Active Silencing & Crash Ledger — 5.5s
- **Visual**: Split glass UI showing Windows 11 system interop:
  - Left: Active Silencer HUD (`WinMMAudioDriver: Master Volume Ducked -70%`).
  - Right: Write-Ahead Crash Ledger badge (`%APPDATA%\HushLoop\silencer-session.json: Protected`).
- **Badge**: `IMMUNE TO POWER LOSS & CRASHES`.
- **Telemetry Counter**: Mini telemetry card animates daily streak (`🔥 Streak: 5 Days`) and focus minutes counting up (`125 mins`).
- **Sequential/interaction**: Silencer badge locks in with shield icon, telemetry numbers count up with CountUp easing.
- **Audio intent**: Crisp lock sound (shield snap) and soft rapid counter ticks.
- **Transition mood**: Dramatic glow dissolve -> Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Cyclical Shift & Grand Finale — 5.5s
- **Visual**: Timer reaches zero. Neon color shifts dynamically from Electric Cyan (`#00F2FE`) to Coral Rose (`#FF4B72`). Status updates to **"SHORT BREAK • RESTORE AUDIO"**.
- **Outro Lockup**: The HushLoop Acoustic Loop logo expands to center.

- **Audio intent**: Resonant harmonic bell chime, followed by music resolving into a warm fade-out.
- **Poster Frame**: Frame at t=17.5s (HushLoop logo lockup with glowing dual-color ring).
